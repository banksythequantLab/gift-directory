Judging the PayPal AI Hackathon? Test it in 5 minutes

Use the sandbox logins from our Devpost "How to test" section. With those sandbox emails every step below runs on the PayPal sandbox and no real money moves. Any PayPal sandbox account works if its email looks like sb-...@personal.example.com.

  1. AI gift finder: open Lawyer Gifts and describe the person in the search bar (e.g. "a litigator who loves coffee, $75"), or scroll to the questions (occasion, who it's for, budget, how you know them) and tap answers. The AI re-picks real, in-stock products, each with a reason.
  2. Group gift with PayPal: on any pick, tap Group gift → enter a name and, as the organizer's PayPal, our sandbox receiver sb-7bf7453357370@personal.example.com (PayPal won't let an account pay itself) → Create the share link. Open the link, chip in with the sandbox buyer, and watch the tally update. The goal includes an estimated shipping & tax amount.
  3. Gift registry: make a registry (use the sandbox receiver above as its PayPal email), ask the AI to fill it, then open the share link in a private window and mark an item "I bought this" or go in together on a big item.
  4. Launch your own store: use the form below → Draft my store → enter the sandbox buyer's email as Your email → Publish, and approve the $19/month PayPal sandbox subscription. Your store goes live at yourname.gift.directory.
  5. AI assistant for owners: from your new store, tap Edit this store → sign in with PayPal (sandbox) → the AI assistant tab plans shelves and applies them when you say so.
