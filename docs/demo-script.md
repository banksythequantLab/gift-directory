# Demo video script — target 2:45 (limit 3:00)

Record at 1280×800 or larger. Everything runs on the PayPal **sandbox**. Use the sandbox buyer account for every payment. Have these tabs open, in order:
1. https://gifts.nota.lawyer (Lawyer Gifts)
2. https://gift.directory (platform)
3. Sandbox inbox / PayPal sandbox login ready in a second browser profile (so you're signed in as the buyer)

Before recording: open each page once so the front-page grid is cached and loads instantly.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Lawyer Gifts front page, slow scroll over the grid | "When a client wants to thank their lawyer, or a team wants to go in on a gift when someone makes partner, it turns into an hour on Amazon and six Venmo requests. This is Lawyer Gifts." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Tap **Made partner** in the question card. The grid re-ranks, and "Picked for you" cards with reasons appear. Tap **The whole litigation team**, then **$200**. | "It opens on real, in-stock gifts from thousands of retailers through Channel3. Instead of a questionnaire, questions sit between the products. Every tap re-ranks the grid, and the AI explains each pick. It can only pick products the catalog actually returned, so there are no made-up items or prices." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Tap **For a judge or clerk** (remove the earlier answers first). The gift-rule notice appears. | "It also knows the profession: judges and court staff are restricted from accepting gifts, so instead of a shopping list you get the rule." |
| 0:55–1:25 | Remove that answer, then click **Group gift** on a pick. Fill in a title, your name and the organizer's sandbox PayPal email, then create. Show the share page. | "Going in together? Start a group gift on any product and share one link. Contributions go straight to the organizer's PayPal account. We never hold the money." |
| 1:25–1:55 | In the buyer browser, open the share link and chip in **$40**. PayPal sandbox checkout, then **Pay**. Back on the page: $40 raised, your note in "Who's in". | "Each person chips in with PayPal Checkout. When they come back, we capture the payment and the tally moves, exactly once, even if they refresh." |
| 1:55–2:30 | Switch to **gift.directory**. Type "nurses and the patients who want to thank them" and click **Draft my store**. Edit the name, show the live preview. | "And it's not just lawyers. Anyone can launch a gift store for their people. Say who it's for, and the AI drafts the store: name, address, occasions and look. Change anything." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Click **Publish — $19/month with PayPal**, approve the sandbox subscription, and land on the live store with the "Your store is live" banner. | "Publish with a PayPal subscription and it's live at its own address. gift.directory: AI finds the gift, and PayPal makes giving it together easy." |

**Notes**
- If "Publish" lands on the "PayPal hasn't confirmed yet" message, reload once; sandbox can lag a few seconds.
- If a Channel3 result looks odd on camera, tap another answer and the grid re-ranks.
